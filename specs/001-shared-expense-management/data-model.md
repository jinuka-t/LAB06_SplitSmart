# Data Model Proposal

**Status**: Proposed; product-policy choices are not approvals
**Source of truth**: Shared service and PostgreSQL; details in [architecture.md](./architecture.md).

## Conventions and ownership

- Use opaque UUID identifiers generated by the service. IDs are stable across clients and do not encode email, group, or other private data.
- Every record is owned by a server-side account or group. Authenticate from the identity-provider subject; never trust a client-supplied owner or role.
- Store money as exact `NUMERIC`/Java `BigDecimal` with currency code and explicit scale. Do not use binary floating-point. Preserve the supported currency/scale used when a financial record was accepted.
- Persist timestamps as UTC instants and business/report dates as `LocalDate`. Report start/end dates are inclusive.
- Prefer soft removal/revocation for memberships and identities referenced by financial history. Financial source records and audit decisions are append-only after effective approval/payment.
- All mutations that affect one financial command, its ledger postings, balance projection, and outbox record share one database transaction.

## Entities

| Entity | Key fields | Relationships / cardinality | Constraints and lifecycle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Account** | `accountId`, `identitySubject`, verified email reference, created/status timestamps | One account may have 0..* memberships, submitted expenses, approvals, notifications, and payments. | Provider subject unique; email/password, verification and recovery are owned by the OIDC provider boundary, not persisted as plaintext by SplitSmart. Disable/revoke access without erasing financial references. |
| **Group** | `groupId`, `name`, `currencyCode`, `currencyScale`, `createdBy`, `status`, `version` | One group has 1..* memberships and 0..* invitations, expenses, payments, policy versions, and notifications. | MVP proposal: one currency per group. Currency must be in approved registry; no default scale assumption. Group is the authorization and report boundary. |
| **Membership** | `(groupId, accountId)`, role(s), accepted/removed timestamps, status, version | Many-to-many join between Group and Account. An active membership may be referenced as submitter, payer, participant, sender, recipient, or approver. | Invitation acceptance creates active membership; pending invitations grant no member access. Revocation does not delete history. Role vocabulary and removal-history access need product approval. |
| **Invitation** | `invitationId`, `groupId`, normalized target email, inviter, token digest, issued/expiry/accepted/revoked timestamps, state | One group issues 0..* invitations; one accepted invitation creates at most one membership. | States `PENDING`, `ACCEPTED`, `EXPIRED`, `REVOKED`; one-use and target identity/email must match. Token material is not stored in clear text. |
| **ApprovalPolicyVersion** | `policyVersionId`, `groupId`, version, stage count/order, active flag, effective timestamps | Group has 1..* historical versions and one active version. A version has 1..* stages; each stage names one or more eligible approvers. | Snapshot policy version when expense is submitted. MVP has one stage; product can configure multiple stages. Who selects approvers and quorum rules remain OD-2. |
| **Expense** | `expenseId`, `groupId`, submitter ID, payer ID, amount, currency/scale, expense date, description, split method, receipt ID?, state, policy version, version, timestamps | One expense has 1..* ordered participant shares; 0..* approval decisions; 0..* receipt references (spec asks optional image; proposal uses at most one); 0..* ledger postings only after final approval. | Payer is a group member but independent of participants. Amount > 0. States `PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`; only final approval creates postings. Submitted input/share snapshot is immutable; resubmission is a new submission or explicit version. |
| **ExpenseParticipantShare** | `expenseId`, `accountId`, `entryOrder`, percentage?; calculated share | Expense has 1..* rows, each for one distinct group member. | Preserve entry order; distinct participant per expense; share >= 0; exact sum equals expense amount at currency scale. Percentages are 0..100 and sum exactly 100 for percentage split. |
| **ApprovalDecision** | `approvalId`, `expenseId`, `policyVersionId`, stage, approver, decision, comment?, timestamp, request key | Expense has 0..* decisions; each decision refers to an eligible approver at a required stage. | Decision `APPROVE` or `REJECT`; one effective decision per approver/stage/expense. Actor must be authorized at decision time; submitter self-approval is disabled by default assumption. Rejected expense never posts. |
| **Receipt** | `receiptId`, `groupId`, uploader, object key, media type, size, checksum, state, created timestamp | Optional receipt attached to one expense; stored bytes reside behind private storage adapter. | Validate allowed image media types/size, scan as deployment requires; private by default. Retrieval requires current authorization to the linked group/expense. |
| **LedgerPosting** | `postingId`, `groupId`, source type/id, debit account, credit account, amount, currency/scale, effective timestamp | Expense can produce 0..n pairwise postings on final approval; payment produces one offsetting posting. | Immutable; unique source key prevents double posting. No self-posting. Each pairwise movement is exact at currency scale. Posting directions are defined once and reused by projection/report code. |
| **PairwiseBalance** | `(groupId, debtorId, creditorId)`, signed/positive amount, currency/scale, version, updated timestamp | Derived projection of ledger postings for a group member pair. | Non-negative outstanding direction per row; do not retain simultaneous opposite-direction rows after normalization. Update atomically with source postings. Can be rebuilt and reconciled from ledger. |
| **Settlement** | `paymentId`, `groupId`, sender ID, recipient ID, amount, currency/scale, payment date, status, idempotency key, created timestamp | One settlement references two distinct group members and one ledger posting when accepted. | Amount > 0; sender must owe recipient at commit time; reject amount > current outstanding. `RECORDED` only after atomic write; rejected attempts create no accepted history row. Records user-reported payment, not a money transfer. |
| **Notification** | `notificationId`, `groupId`, recipient, event/source IDs, channel, state, created/delivered timestamps, attempts | Group expense event creates 0..* recipient notifications excluding submitter per FR-008. | Outbox intent persisted with business transaction. Consumer deduplicates by ID. Delivery states include `QUEUED`, `DELIVERED`, `RETRYING`, `FAILED`; exact channel, preference and retry policy are OD-4. |
| **ReportQuery/ReportResult** | Group, requester, inclusive start/end dates, criteria, generated timestamp, summary values | A query reads authorized expenses, balances, and settlements; result is ephemeral unless export/audit is later requested. | Reversed ranges rejected. Pending/rejected records do not count as settled expense totals. Date field used is expense date or payment date respectively. |

## Relationships and cardinalities

- `Account 1 — 0..* Membership`; `Group 1 — 1..* Membership`; an active `(group, account)` pair is unique.
- `Group 1 — 0..* Expense`; each expense has exactly one group, submitter, and payer.
- `Expense 1 — 1..* ExpenseParticipantShare`; participant accounts are distinct and in the expense's group.
- `Expense 1 — 0..* ApprovalDecision`; each decision points to a policy version and stage.
- `Expense 0..1 — Receipt`; receipt bytes are outside the relational row.
- `Expense/Settlement 1 — 0..* LedgerPosting`; pending/rejected expense has zero effective postings; successful settlement has one logical balance-offset posting.
- `Group 1 — 0..* PairwiseBalance`; projection rows refer to retained member identities, including historical members.
- `Group 1 — 0..* Notification`; each recipient is an account and is never the expense submitter for submission notices.

## State transitions

| Aggregate | Allowed transitions | Financial effect |
|---|---|---|
| Invitation | `PENDING → ACCEPTED / EXPIRED / REVOKED` | Acceptance creates active membership; before acceptance no group-member authorization. |
| Expense | `DRAFT → PENDING`; `PENDING → APPROVED` only on final required approval; `PENDING → REJECTED` by an authorized approver | Only `PENDING → APPROVED` posts approved shares once. `REJECTED` and `PENDING` have no balance effect. |
| Approval stage | `WAITING → APPROVED` or `WAITING → REJECTED` | Each eligible stage decision is immutable; rejection terminates the expense workflow. |
| Settlement | `REQUESTED/VALIDATING → RECORDED` or rejected before persistence | `RECORDED` offsets outstanding pairwise debt once. Failed/over-limit command has no accepted payment record or balance effect. |
| Notification | `QUEUED → DELIVERED`, `RETRYING`, or `FAILED` | Delivery state never reverses the expense/approval/payment transaction. |

## Validation and calculation rules

1. Check account/group authorization and current membership before reading or writing group records.
2. Reject a missing/invalid payer or any non-member participant; reject empty or duplicate participant lists and non-positive amounts.
3. Validate input precision against the group's supported currency scale. Do not silently round an over-precision input.
4. For percentages, require each percentage in the inclusive 0–100 range and the exact sum 100%; use decimal arithmetic, not tolerance-based floating point.
5. Calculate quotas using `BigDecimal`; round down to whole minor units, allocate leftover units by descending fractional remainder, then `entryOrder` ascending. Assert exact total equality before persistence.
6. Apply expense share obligations only after final approval. A payer who is also a participant has zero debt to self; every other participant owes their calculated share to payer.
7. Check positive settlement amount and current directed balance atomically. Reject overpayment and duplicate request safely.
8. A report's inclusive date boundaries use the record-specific date (`expenseDate`, `paymentDate`); group membership authorization is reevaluated at query time.

## Persistence boundary

PostgreSQL owns identity references, groups, memberships, invitations, policy versions, expense snapshots, approvals, receipt metadata, ledger postings, balance projections, settlements, notifications/outbox, and indexes. Identity-provider secrets and receipt binaries do not belong in domain tables. A local Swing cache, if later approved, is disposable presentation data only; it cannot finalize expenses/payments or override server balance state.
