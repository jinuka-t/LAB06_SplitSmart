## Risks and controls

- Group financial lock is simple and safe but may bottleneck hot groups. Measure NFR-002 before narrowing locks; preserve pair concurrency invariants.
- Projection corruption can hide overpayments: reconcile against immutable ledger, fail closed on detected mismatch, rehearse recovery. SQL transaction rollback and unique keys alone do not replace reconciliation.
- Outbox cannot guarantee mail delivery or user attention. Monitor lag/dead letters, offer recovery resend with abuse limits, define a notification delivery contract before acceptance.
- Policy snapshots preserve review authority but can deadlock progress when eligible reviewers leave. Explicit withdrawal/resubmission preserves audit instead of quietly replacing reviewers.
- Former-member access and irreversible records need privacy/retention decisions. Do not add destructive deletion as a convenience.
- Receipt availability spans SQL and blob storage; staging cleanup and coordinated backup prevent missing attachments from masquerading as valid receipts.
- Saved completion must be authoritative. Offline/timeouts and future replica lag need same-key retries/read watermarks across all clients.
- Traceability is design evidence only. The readiness checklist's ambiguity/testability box remains open; this proposal cannot close it. No application acceptance or human approval is inferred from generated documents.

## Adoption gate

Human reviewers must resolve D01-D05 before treating MVP scope/behavior as approved, then review D06-D10 implementation/acceptance details. Review all artifacts, monetary worked cases and contract/diagram consistency before code. Implementation acceptance later requires relevant JUnit 5 results, accurate rendered PlantUML, permission attacks, accessibility checks, usability observation, load evidence and client parity.
